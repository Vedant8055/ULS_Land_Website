A little more clarity

Considered answers.

Every question deserves a transparent answer, especially at the beginning of an ambitious vision.

Does a preferred date mean my visit is booked?

No. This experience collects a preferred date only. A private site consultation must be explicitly confirmed by the project team after checking site access, safety, and availability.

Can I book a helicopter tour?

Not at this stage. Aviation access is an unconfirmed concept subject to specialist feasibility, safety requirements and regulatory approvals. It is not part of the current enquiry options.

Are the proposed sustainability systems already operational?

This website describes a concept-stage vision. Solar, water reuse, zero-waste systems and other features have different engineering, funding and approval dependencies. Hydrogen is specifically a future feasibility topic and should not be presented as installed infrastructure.

Will my personal details be submitted?

Not in this interactive prototype. The form runs locally and does not send information to a backend. A real launch will need approved privacy notices, secure lead processing and access controls.

Are the images from the actual Masrul site?

No. All photography on this prototype is illustrative and sourced from authentic stock photography, including a Sahyadri regional image. Actual site photographs and architect-approved project renders should replace these when available.
